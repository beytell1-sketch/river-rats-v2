## Open items requiring owner direction or factory agent action

**1. BP6 board isolation (critical flag)**
6 of 10 BP6 situations share boards with other sub-patterns. The brief
explicitly prohibits this. Factory agent must design 3-4 additional BP6-only
boards before generating situations. The board architect has flagged which
BP6 failure modes need new boards (Section 8 of the delivery document).

**2. Paired board missing (R2 texture)**
Zero paired flop boards designed (R2 requires minimum 1). Add one paired board
(e.g., 5c-5d-Ah or 7c-7h-Kd) — both card sets appear clear of the 79-board
prior inventory but must be verified before use.

**3. SPR variation is situation-level, not board-level**
All boards are set at SPR 10.8 (standard SRP flop). The brief requires
situations at SPR 3.0-8.0 and 1.5-3.0 as well. These must be generated by
the factory agent through effective_stack variation per situation row, not
through additional board designs. Section 8 specifies the required ranges
per sub-pattern.

**4. BP2 board count**
4 unique boards vs minimum 5 required by R6. The 4th board (B4_13) covers
the turn variant. A 5th BP2 board (e.g., a second A-high rainbow flop) can
be added by the factory agent if needed.

**5. BP5 board count**
3 unique boards vs minimum 4. A 4th low rainbow board (e.g., 7c-3s-2h) can
be added during factory generation.
