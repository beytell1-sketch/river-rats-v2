### MW-31

**Original action sequence:** "BB checks, hero (BTN) bets 30, CO raises to 90. BB folds."

**Error:**
(1) BTN bets before CO has acted in the initiative round. Postflop initiative order is BB(1) → CO(4) → BTN(5). CO must check or bet before BTN acts.
(2) After BTN bets, clockwise-from-bettor response order is BB first, then CO. The original sequence has CO raising before BB folds — that is wrong. BB must respond before CO.

**Corrected action sequence:** BB checks, CO checks, BTN bets 30, BB folds, CO raises to 90. Hero (BTN) faces CO's raise.

**Validator output:**
```
VALID
```
Command run:
```
python3 river-rats-core/hand_sequence_validator.py \
  --action "BB check, CO check, BTN bet 30, BB fold, CO raise 90" \
  --positions BB,CO,BTN --street flop --hero BTN
```

**Positions:** BB, CO, BTN
**Hero position:** BTN — CLOSING ACTION (last in initiative round; last remaining to respond after BB folds)
**Pot / Bet / To call:** Pot 210 / Raise to 90 / To call 60

Pot math with corrected sequence:
- Preflop pot: 90 (CO opens, BTN calls, BB calls)
- BTN bets 30 → pot 120
- BB folds (no additional chips)
- CO raises to 90 → pot 120 + 90 = 210
- Hero must call 60 more (already invested 30)
- Pot if hero calls: 270

**Pot odds:** 60 / (210 + 60) = 60 / 270 = **22.2%**

The pot math is identical to the original spec. BB folded without putting additional chips in regardless of whether BB folded before or after CO raised. The action-order correction does not change the pot size at hero's decision point.

**Old label:** FOLD — GTO OVERRIDE (from BATCH2_8_HAND_DESIGNS.md)
**Re-evaluated label:** FOLD — HIGH confidence

**Label changed?** NO

**Reasoning:** The corrected sequence changes only the order of BB fold and CO raise — it does not change hero's decision context. Hero (BTN) holds AsJs (TPJK, top pair with the jack kicker on AQ5r) and faces a check-raise from CO after BTN bet into a 3-way pot.

The game state at hero's decision is identical:
- Hero is now heads-up vs CO (BB folded)
- CO check-raised the flop in a multiway pot
- Pot 210, to call 60, pot odds 22.2%
- Hero's raw equity is 0.653 per pipeline output

CO's check-raise range on AQ5r is the most polarized, strength-weighted action available. CO must beat both the bettor (BTN) and the remaining player (BB) with a check-raise in a 3-way pot. That range is essentially: AQ (two pair, 9 combos), sets of QQ (3) and 55 (3), occasional AA slowplay (3-6 combos), and AK (TPGK dominating hero's AsJs). Hero's AsJs (TPJK) loses to every combo in this range or is dominated by AK. Pipeline equity of 0.653 reflects CO's entire preflop opening range — not the narrow subset that check-raises in a multiway pot against a position bet. This is the canonical Axis 5 (Aggression Respect) lesson: a check-raise in a multiway pot is the strongest range signal in poker. FOLD is correct.

Positional classification of BTN is unchanged: BTN was CLOSING ACTION in the initiative round (acted last after BB and CO both checked). After BTN bets, BTN becomes the last to respond to CO's raise (having already bet, now facing the re-raise). No positional reclassification — hero is still IP vs CO.

**Solver verification needed?** NO — The FOLD label is established by GTO OVERRIDE in the design spec with explicit reasoning. The action-order correction does not change the pot odds, the hero's hand, CO's check-raise range, or the game-state at decision time. The label stands.

**Pot / Bet / To call:** Pot 90 / No bet / No call

**Old label:** BET — HIGH confidence (from calibration_answers_batch_2.md)

**Label changed?** NO

**Reasoning:** The correction removes an illegal BTN action from before hero's turn. It does not change any material game-state factor:

- Hero still holds AcAd (overpair) as the preflop opener (CO)
- Board is still J94r (dry, static, rainbow)
- BB still checked before hero
- BTN has not yet acted (they will act after hero, not before)
- Pot is still 90

The positional classification changes slightly in description: in the original (incorrect) sequence, it appeared hero was closing action (last to act) because both BB and BTN had checked. In the corrected sequence, hero is second in initiative order with BTN still to act behind. This makes hero's position slightly worse, not better — there is one player behind.

Does this change the BET label? No. The factors supporting BET are unchanged and overwhelming:
- AcAd is a near-nut overpair (only JJ, 99, 44 beat hero = ~3% of villain ranges per pipeline: better_hand_pct 0.05)
- Villain_air_pct 0.44 — high air fraction supports value betting
- Both callers have capped preflop ranges (no premium overpairs in BB or BTN flat ranges)
- Board is J94r — static, dry, no flush or straight draws materializing
- Equity 0.666 against combined opponent ranges
- Villain_aggression_count 0; villain_checked_back (BB confirmed weakness)

The one wrinkle is BTN acting after hero. If hero bets, BTN can raise. But BTN's raising range on J94r after checking is extremely narrow — only sets (JJ/99/44) and occasional slow-played AA (which CO holds, removing those combos). The risk of a BTN raise does not outweigh the value of betting a near-nut overpair on a dry board with high villain air. Check-back would give free equity to draws (any suited connector hands) and underpairs that would fold to a bet.

BET is still correct. Hero should bet small (25-33% pot) for value, consistent with the original label.

**Solver verification needed?** NO — BET with AA on J94r is unambiguous. The action-order correction (BTN acting after, not before, hero) is a minor contextual clarification that does not affect the BET decision for a near-nut overpair on a dry board.
