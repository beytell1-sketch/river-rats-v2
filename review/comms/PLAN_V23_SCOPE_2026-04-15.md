## Section 2: Defensive Multiway-Checked-Through CHECK Bias

### Source Material

- 10 MW reference misses from `review/comms/SOLVER_VERIFICATION_MW_MISSES_2026-04-15.html`
- Stream B.2 GTO analysis: `review/comms/MW_MISS_BIAS_ANALYSIS_2026-04-15.md`
- Evidence pack: `review/comms/MW_MISS_EVIDENCE_PACK_2026-04-15.md` (commit `3b69360`)
- 4 d-series solver mixed spots from `training-data/solver_verification_log.jsonl`:
  d4312_CO_turn, d8886_BTN_flop, d8963_HJ_turn, BP5_01

### Adopted Bias Wording (Stream B.2, verbatim)

> **Defensive multiway-checked-through CHECK bias.** The v2.2
> model underbets in multiway pots where villain(s) have
> checked the previous street, villain ranges are capped, hero
> sits at or above median range strength with worse_hand_pct
> ≥ 0.55, and SPR is low (≤ 2). The model reads mutual
> passivity plus villain_top_pair_plus density as
> range-vs-range standoff and defaults to pot-control CHECK,
> overriding the value+protection case that the passive
> villain line actually enables. The v2.3 supplement should
> target this bucket specifically; a uniform "bet more often"
> correction would overshoot.

### Precondition Predicate (measurable)

The supplement targeting predicate — the exact feature-condition
conjunction that must hold for a spot to qualify as being inside
the bias bucket:

```
facing_bet = False
∧ num_opponents ≥ 2
∧ villain_checked_back = 1
∧ villain_range_capped = 1
∧ worse_hand_pct ≥ 0.55
∧ equity_vs_range ≥ 0.35
∧ SPR ≤ 2.0
```

Each predicate term maps to a feature in the 37-feature vector and
is directly measurable at both training-data construction time and
evaluation time. The v2.3 supplement, the diagnostic test set
(Track E), and any training-CSV spot-check (Stream C) all use this
same predicate for consistency.

### Why This Wording Was Chosen Over "Bucket-First CHECK Bias"

The earlier framing ("bucket-first CHECK bias", anchored on
`hero_range_percentile = 0.00`) was too broad: it implied the
model overchecks generally. The Stream B.2 evidence (see table in
`MW_MISS_BIAS_ANALYSIS_2026-04-15.md` Q4) shows the bias is
narrower and conditional. HRP across the 10 misses ranges 0.43–
0.83 (mean 0.64), i.e. hero is often mid-range, not bottom-of-
range — the "HRP=0.00 anchor" was a test-harness artifact (see
`HRP_INVESTIGATION_2026-04-15.md`). The true signature is the
**mutual-passivity × capped-villain-range × low-SPR conjunction**
above, not a trap-lean on top-of-range holdings alone.

Q3 of the Stream B.2 analysis establishes that labellers produced
the correct BET override on these spots (citing villain weakness +
capped range + high worse_hand_pct); the failure is that the model
did not learn the conditional override — it is a training-data
sparsity / model-capacity problem, not label conservatism. The
v2.3 supplement targets the bucket directly; the prompt override
(below) reduces panel-vote variance on boundary spots.

### Pass 1 Prompt Override Clause (secondary fix, verbatim)

The following clause is to be inserted into `gto_labeller_v3.md`
(precise insertion point covered in Section 3). Source:
`MAIN_TERMINAL_UPDATE_2026-04-15-f.md` §2.2, verbatim.

> When villain_checked_back=1, villain_range_capped=1,
> num_opponents≤2 (or specifically ≥2 in MW context), and
> hero's worse_hand_pct exceeds 0.55, prefer BET for
> value+protection even when OOP or holding a medium-strength
> made hand. The passive line forfeits the capped villain's
> air portion.

## Section 4: Solver Ratio for v2.3

### Target

15-20% of 591 hands = 90-120 hands with solver verification.

### Structure: Queue-Based Triggers

v2.3 uses a queue-based approach rather than a single batch.
Reason: the 206-hand supplement is generated and labelled in
multiple small batches (the same 4+2 team structure as v2.2).
Solver verification happens as batches complete, not after all
206 are labelled.

**Auto-enqueue triggers.** A hand is automatically added to the
solver verification queue when any of the following fires:

1. **Confidence LOW on any label.** Pass 1 or Pass 2 agent assigns
   confidence = LOW. No exception — LOW-confidence hands go to
   solver.

2. **Mixed-strategy flag in Pass 2 review.** A Pass 2 reviewer
   notes that two or more Pass 1 agents disagreed AND the split
   was 2-2 (not 3-1). 2-2 splits are genuine mixed-strategy
   candidates. Enqueue automatically.

3. **Overrides a unanimous Pass 1 panel.** Per v2.3 backlog item 8
   (reframed in Section 6): any single-panelist Pass 2 override of
   a 3/4-majority Pass 1 decision must be solver-verified before
   the override stands. This is the BP1_08 / d1983 lesson.

4. **BET-in-mixed-zone hand from supplement.** Any supplement hand
   in the "Mixed-zone BET (solver-confirmed)" row of Section 1's
   allocation table is sourced directly from solver. These are
   pre-queued — they are not labelled by agents first; the solver
   output IS the label.

5. **~~hero_range_percentile = 0.00 with visibly strong hand.**~~ [corrected — see HRP_INVESTIGATION_2026-04-15.md: hrp=0 was a test-harness artifact, NOT part of the bias signature]
   Once the feature audit (prompted by Addition B in Section 3)
   confirms which situations trigger the floor-value artifact,
   all affected hands in the supplement are enqueued for spot-
   check.

**Manual enqueue.** Owner may enqueue any hand at any point,
regardless of triggers. No approval needed.

### Owner Time Budget

Solver verification of one 3-way hand in GTO Wizard takes
approximately 5-8 minutes including pre-flight (sequence
validation, bet-size matching per `feedback_solver_preflight.md`
and `feedback_solver_aligned_sizing.md`). At 90-120 hands target:

| Session length | Hands verified per session | Sessions needed (90-hand target) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | 4-5 | 18-23 |
| 60 min | 7-10 | 9-13 |
| 90 min | 11-15 | 6-8 |

Recommendation: 2-3 dedicated 60-minute solver sessions per week
during v2.3 labelling. The queue-based triggers mean hands arrive
in a steady stream rather than all at once.

### Batch vs Queue Decision

Use the queue. Do not do a single end-of-phase solver batch as was
done in Phase 3.5G. Reasons:
- Early solver feedback catches systematic prompt errors before
  they affect the full 206-hand supplement.
- The 10 MW misses (Section 2) show that a late-stage solver batch
  reveals problems too late to fix cheaply.
- Owner time is constrained; spreading verification across the
  labelling phase is lower-risk than front-loading or back-loading it.

## Section 6: v2.2 Lessons Applied

### Pass 2 Override Discipline (Backlog Item 8 Reframed)

v2.2 produced two documented cases where Pass 2 overrode a
strong-majority Pass 1 result and was subsequently reversed by
solver:

- BP1_08: Pass 1 was 3/4 RAISE. Pass 2 overrode to CALL.
  Solver confirmed RAISE. Reverted.
- d1983: Pass 1 was 4/4 BET (unanimous). Pass 2 overrode to CHECK
  (incorrectly applied Tier 5 flag). Solver confirmed BET. Reverted.

The reframed rule for v2.3 is:

> A Pass 2 panelist MAY override a Pass 1 majority result only when
> they can cite a specific KB section, a pattern from the
> calibration anchors, or a feature-value condition that the Pass 1
> panel demonstrably failed to consider. Citing a general "feels
> passive" or "SPR concern" without a KB reference is not sufficient
> grounds for override. When overriding a 3/4 or 4/4 Pass 1 result,
> the overriding panelist must enqueue the hand for solver
> verification (per the auto-enqueue trigger in Section 4). If solver
> disagrees with the override, the override is reverted. If solver
> is unavailable, the override is provisionally held but flagged in
> the assembly CSV as DISPUTED_OVERRIDE.

This rule is operationalized as a field in the Pass 2 review
template: `override_kb_justification` (required when action
diverges from Pass 1 majority) and `enqueue_for_solver` (boolean,
auto-set to true on 3/4+ overrides).

### No New Architecture Phases

v2.3 uses the same 4+2 team structure as v2.2:
- 4 independent labelling panels (Pass 1)
- 2 Pass 2 review panels
- 1 assembly step (Phase 3.5H equivalent)
- 1 training step (Phase 4 equivalent)

No new phases, no new agent types, no pipeline restructuring.
The v2.2 pipeline demonstrated that the architecture works — the
failures were calibration and data distribution problems, not
structural problems.

The one structural addition is the queue-based solver integration
(Section 4), which replaces the end-of-phase solver batch. This
is a process change within existing Phase 3.5G, not a new phase.

### Solver Use Policy

Solver is used in v2.3 for three purposes only:

1. **Pattern detection.** Analyzing the 10 MW misses and d-series
   spots to identify the empirical bias signature (Section 2). This
   is retrospective analysis, not per-hand arbitration.

2. **Clear-wrong escalations.** When a hand is queued via auto-
   enqueue triggers and the solver shows a clear, unambiguous
   result (e.g. 85%+ BET frequency), the solver output overrides
   the panel label. Clear-wrong means the solver output is outside
   the mixed-strategy zone.

3. **Mixed-strategy sourcing.** The 10-20 solver-sourced hands in
   the supplement allocation table (Section 1) are solver-verified
   by design — the solver output IS the label.

Solver is NOT used for:
- Per-hand arbitration of all labelled hands
- Validating pass/fail on calibration exam hands (those are ground-
  truthed once and locked, not re-run each session)
- Resolving difficulty-2 disagreements where both actions have
  real arguments (these stay at the panel consensus unless an
  auto-enqueue trigger fires)

This policy keeps the solver budget within the Section 4 owner
time estimates and prevents the Solver Becomes the Labeller failure
mode documented in `feedback_solver_vs_expert_labels.md`.

### Action Distribution Work is Out of Scope

v3.0 Action Distributions (gauge labels, soft targets, action_lean
field) are documented in `review/comms/V23_BACKLOG_ACTION_DISTRIBUTIONS_2026-04-14.md`.
That work requires a mature solver verification pipeline and
deployed v2.2 oracle. It is not part of v2.3 scope. Do not add
the `action_lean` optional field to gto_labeller_v3.md in this
version — collect baseline data first, experiment in v3.0.
