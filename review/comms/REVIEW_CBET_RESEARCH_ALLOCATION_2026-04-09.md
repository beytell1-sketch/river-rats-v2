## Finding

The plan allocates "≥2 research agents + 1 reviewer" for c-bet
research. This meets the §1.2 minimum but violates §1.1:

**§1.1: "Research agents: 1 topic per agent. Never combine."**

The plan lists 5 distinct research subtopics:
1. C-bet frequency by PFA vs defender
2. Board texture effects on c-betting
3. Multiway c-bet sizing
4. When to check back (PFA and defender)
5. Blocker effects on c-bet decisions

Two agents covering 5 topics means at least one agent handles 2-3
topics in a single context. That's the pattern §1.1 explicitly
prohibits.

## Recommendation

Allocate 5 research agents (1 per subtopic) + 3 reviewers
(≥ researcher count ÷ 2). All 5 can run in parallel per §1.3.

The builder should update the Phase B team plan before launching.
