  CALL: standard default with a nut flush draw at reasonable price. Pot

    and flush outs. Profitable call with realization.
  RAISE: KB 1.7 semi-bluff conditions. Nut draw ✓. Ace blocker ✓.

    folded, so this is now effectively HU. The "3-way semi-bluff

    before hero acts). Solver raise frequency for AsXs-type nut-draw
    holdings HU on two-tone K-high boards facing a pot-size c-bet is
    high (often 50%+ when you have the Ace blocker + backdoor
    side-equity).

Step 4 — choose: RAISE is best on merit. KB 1.7 all-four conditions are
  met: nut draw, blocker, side equity, and position/flow are favourable
  (one opponent already gone, hero closing HU with a nut draw + blocker).
  Even with no side equity beyond an ace overcard, solver supports raise
  here.
Alt evaluated: CALL is close (it is the default non-set mixed-label
  instruction in KB 1.7). However, the combination of BTN already
  folding + Ace blocker + nut flush draw on a two-tone board pushes

HAND 2 — FB-24
================================================================================

Hero: Ah Kc    Board: Ad 9c 3h 2s Kd (river)    Position: BTN (3-way)
Villains: BB (bettor), CO (folded)
Action: (flop/turn undisclosed) → river BB bets 90 into 120, CO fold,
hero to act. Pot 210, to call 90. Pot odds 90/(120+90+90) = 30%.

HOLDOUT: RAISE    v2.3.2: CALL

Step 1 — bucket: STRONG MADE (two pair, top and second-pair, A+K on
  A93-2-K). The K river gave hero top two pair. This is a very strong
  made hand on this runout.

Step 2 — situation: River, BTN closing action, facing lead from BB
  after CO folded. Action is HU effectively. The King on the river is a
  brutal card for BB's range if BB was leading/calling earlier — A9,
  AK, A3, A2, 99, 33, 22, K-something, any sets (999/333/222) fills up,
  KK fills up. Specifically: sets of 9s/3s/2s now have full houses (99
  full of Ks, 33 full of Ks, 22 full of Ks), and AKs is possible in
  BB's preflop range (premium combos in BB flat/overcall). Also KK
  filled up on river. Straight-wise A2345 / 23456 cannot fall (no T
  needed actually wait — impossible). Wheel: A2345 would need a 4 or 5,
  neither there. So straights are out.

  So better hands in BB's river-betting range: AA (3 combos), 99/33/22
  (3 combos of sets that rivered boats), AK (3 combos of 2-pair now
  tied or above — actually AK is TIE with hero's AK, not better), KK
  (if BB leads it after checking turn — possible), AKs fills tied.

  Full houses BB can credibly have leading river: 9x full (99), 3x
  full (33), 2x full (22), KK, AA.
  Two-pair ties: AK combos. But hero blocks 2 of those.
  Worse value BB could lead: A9 (A9s in overcall range), A3s, A2s,
    some Kx that turned pair — but those rarely lead river after
    checking earlier; this looks like a polarised river lead.

Step 3 — actions:
  FOLD: Way too strong to fold top two pair on this board for 30% pot
    odds. Reject.
  CALL: Standard value-catch with top two pair. Hero beats Ax two-pair
    tries, A-with-kicker, bluffs with missed draws.
  RAISE: For value, hoping to get called by worse (A9, A3s, AKo ties
    fund hero's raise with tied combos). But BB's river lead range is
    polarised — worse hands rarely call a raise, better hands (full
    houses) crush it.

  KB Factor 5 check-raise signal: BB LEADS the river, not a
    check-raise. BB's unsolicited river lead into the PFA after the
    board pairs is NOT the "check-raise = nuts" pattern (that's DO NOT
    Rule #3 for raising — doesn't apply directly). But BB lead on a
    paired river into two opponents still polarises heavily toward
    nuts or air.

Step 4 — choose: CALL is the correct action. Top two pair is a clear
  bluff-catcher vs BB's polarised river lead. Raising for value folds
  out bluffs and gets called / raised by better.

Alternatives: RAISE rejected — turns hand into a bluff catcher only
  facing re-raise; no worse value-hand calls.
FOLD rejected — equity vs bluffs + thin value comfortably beats
  30% pot odds.

v3.1 judgment: CALL. Confidence: HIGH.

Classification vs holdout (RAISE) and v2.3.2 (CALL):
  v3.1 agrees with v2.3.2.
  Holdout RAISE is poker-incorrect here: BB's river lead range on a
  board that pairs the PFA's top card is too polarised to raise for

  to CALL.

CLASSIFICATION: **CORRECTION**    Confidence: HIGH
Rationale: v2.2-era holdout label is poker-stale. v2.3.2 predicts the

HAND 4 — FB-40
================================================================================

Hero: 6s 5s    Board: Kc 8c 4d (flop)    Position: BB (3-way)
Villains: BTN (bettor), CO (checked)
Action: BB check, CO check, BTN bet 30 into 90 (1/3 pot), BB to act.
Pot 120, to call 30. Pot odds 30/(90+30+30) = 20%.

HOLDOUT: FOLD    v2.3.2: CALL

  draw (7 makes the wheel — wait, 6547 would need 7 for 45678, 5-high
  straight needs A2345 no wait 6s5s on K-8-4: need 7-6-5-4-X? let's
  re-check. 6-5 with 4 on board. Outs to make a straight: 7 (for 4-5-6-7-X
  needing one more, actually: 3-4-5-6-7 needs a 3 AND 7, or 4-5-6-7-8
  needs 7 and already has 8). So 7 completes 4-5-6-7-8 straight (inside
  straight draw / gutshot using 8 on board). That's 4 outs (four 7s).
  Also 6s5s has backdoor flush draw (spades). Pair outs: 5, 6 (pairing
  might give 2 pair but bottom pairs aren't really value). Clubs on
  board means no club outs. Total real outs: 4 gutshot outs.
  This is a BACKDOOR/GUTSHOT air hand — KB 1.7 explicitly: "gutshot-only
  or backdoor-only = check/fold."

Step 2 — situation: BTN bets 30 into 90 (small 33% sizing) after CO
  checked. Very small bet is BTN's range probe on K-high. BB must close
  with a speculative hand. Pot odds 20%. Hero equity with 4 gutshot
  outs = 4/47 ≈ 8.5% one card, ~16% by river (if they continued)…
  but hero can't see river for free from OOP.

Step 3 — actions:
  FOLD: KB 1.7 explicit — gutshot only / backdoor-only = check/fold.
    Hero is OOP, no made hand, 4 outs, no nut draw.
  CALL: 20% pot odds vs ~9% immediate equity is a clear fold on raw
    math. Implied odds argument: on completion (a 7), hero makes a
    disguised straight and can extract. But flush completes on turn
    for BTN (two clubs on board). Plus facing CO still to come
    potentially — actually CO checked, so they're essentially capped
    weak, but could still float.
    Implied odds are poor: OOP, small stack leverage, BTN polarised
    small-bet range.
  RAISE: preposterous with no draw equity, no fold equity 3-way.

Step 4 — choose: FOLD. Cleanly fits KB 1.7 "gutshot-only = check/fold"
  and DO NOT Rule #2 ("don't barrel/continue with gutshots 3-way").
  OOP in a 3-way pot with 4 outs, even at 20% pot odds, is a losing
  continuation when you factor EQR (~60-80% OOP in BB = realised
  equity below needed threshold) and the presence of the non-folded CO
  behind (who called a check + could check-raise).

Alternatives:
  CALL rejected — KB 1.7 explicit; implied odds insufficient OOP with
    only 4 outs; danger of further action.
  RAISE rejected — no fold equity, no hand.

v3.1 judgment: FOLD. Confidence: HIGH.

Classification vs holdout (FOLD) and v2.3.2 (CALL):
  v3.1 agrees with holdout FOLD. v2.3.2 is wrong.
  This is a REGRESSION — v2.3.2 is loosening the BB cold-calling range
  into a gutshot-only spot against a 33%-pot bet. KB 1.7 is explicit.

CLASSIFICATION: **REGRESSION**    Confidence: HIGH
Rationale: Classic KB 1.7 violation in v2.3.2. The fold is the
  textbook-correct action for a gutshot-only, OOP, non-nut draw.
